Methodology & known gaps

This pipeline reconstructs the six factors from historical log rows, not from a live request — a few of them are necessarily approximations of what the live MITM would compute in the moment.

  • F_time is estimated from the requestor's country (and, for a handful of large countries, region) rather than Cloudflare's own per-IP geolocation timezone — large countries spanning multiple zones (the US, Russia, Canada, Australia, Brazil) are the most likely to be misjudged near band edges.
  • F_name reuses a reverse-DNS answer for up to 7 days rather than looking it up fresh every time, so a requestor's name factor can lag a recent PTR change.
  • F_AS and F_name both fall back to their unrated defaults (1.0 / 1.25) when a run has too many new requestors to look all of them up in time — those rows are flagged internally and topped up on a later run.
  • Bot Management score, WAF attack score, and JA4 are stored for comparison only and never feed the formula.
  • Source identity is deliberately one-way. This pipeline queries every zone on the account (http_requests turns out to be zone-scoped, not queryable account-wide), and most of those zones are real, unrelated sites — not evilbyte.net. What lands in "By source" is a SHA-256 hash of the zone's Cloudflare-internal id, truncated to 10 hex characters: stable enough to show traffic patterns per source over time, not reversible to which real site it is. Nothing else identifying a specific zone (its name, hostname) is requested, stored, or logged anywhere in this pipeline.
  • A zone this token can't query for logs (a permissions gap for that specific zone) is skipped for that run, not fatal to the others — see src/logs.js.
