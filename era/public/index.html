§6 · the evil rating authority

era.evilbyte.net

The central authority Section 6 describes: a published multiplier, F_AS, for every Autonomous System. No live Elo history runs behind these numbers — nobody's reporting real exchanges via ESRP (§6.4) — so what's served here is each AS's initial rating (§6.1), seeded per Section 4.2's own exceptions and a few pattern rules of the same kind, run across every allocated ASN on the Internet.
