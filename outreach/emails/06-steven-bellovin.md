**Subject:** A very belated sequel to the evil bit

Hi Professor Bellovin,

This is a fan letter more than anything else. [RFC
3514](https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc3514) is one of the reasons I think
April 1 RFCs are worth taking seriously as a form, and I've just finished
writing what amounts to its sequel:
[draft-traviss-evil-byte-00](https://evilbyte.net/draft.html), "The Evil
Byte," which formally obsoletes the evil bit on the grounds that (a) nobody
ever set it honestly and (b) one bit was never going to be enough resolution
anyway. It replaces it with a full eight-bit rating computed by a
middlebox, feeding a central Elo-rated authority for every Autonomous
System.

You're credited in the Acknowledgements, for reasons that should be
obvious. I'm not asking you for anything — no review, no sponsorship, no
time commitment — I just didn't want to publish something that leans this
heavily on your bit without you knowing it exists before it does. If you
ever have five minutes and feel like reading Section 6 (the Elo system) or
Section 9 (what happens when the MITM has to intercept a pigeon), I'd be
glad to hear what you think. If not, no reply needed — I just wanted you to
have seen it first.

It's version -00, unsubmitted, and — per [the
roadmap](https://evilbyte.net/rfc-process.html) — currently sitting at
exactly the stage where it needs readers before it needs anything else.

With thanks for the original,
Rose Traviss
